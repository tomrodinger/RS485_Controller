# Motor connector-view evidence and remaining boundary

The motor documentation and revision 1.5 J4 pad nets establish six electrical contacts: 1 GND, 2 motor V+, 3 A, 4 B, 5 A, 6 B. The documented pigtail colors are black, red, green, blue, green, blue. These facts support the functional keys used in the selected diagrams.

The [official JST PH-series drawing](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/ePH.pdf) identifies the six-circuit S6B-PH-K-S side-entry family and depicts the circuit-1 mark and mounting-side reference views. It supports connector family geometry; it does not by itself identify the installed viewing direction of a finished Gearotons motor. The motor's dimension reference names S6B-PH-K(LF)(SN), so exact variant/housing details should not be silently substituted from a different suffix.

Additional owner photographs were inspected under `../Servomotor/Pictures/servomotor3/M17-48 motor kit/` and `../Servomotor/Pictures/servomotor3/M17-48 motor/`. A rear-label photograph named `微信图片_20250714153946.jpg` shows the GND / positive / A / B / A / B order on its rear panel, but the photographed unit is labelled **M3-48**, despite its directory name. It is therefore not sufficient evidence for claiming a current M17 cable-exit/mating-face orientation. The kit photographs also show separate loose pigtails rather than an unambiguous installed keyed connector from the required view.

Result: retain detached motor pigtail function keys. A future literal mating-face detail should be checked against a current physical M17 sample or an unambiguous current connector photograph. The selected gallery drawings do not assign individual colored leads to unverified socket cavities.
